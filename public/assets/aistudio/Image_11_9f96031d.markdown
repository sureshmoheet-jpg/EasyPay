## Brand & Style

This design system expresses speed, unquestioned security, and frictionless everyday utility for peer-to-peer and merchant payments. It blends high-clarity modern utility with a vibrant, tactile digital wallet personality.

### Target Audience & Emotional Stance
- **Primary Audience:** Mobile-first digital natives, daily commuters, and everyday consumers executing peer-to-peer transfers, QR code checkouts, utility bills, and bank-direct settlements.
- **Emotional Mandate:** Immediate reassurance at transaction commitment, instant recognition of monetary value changes, and zero cognitive strain during multi-step authentication.
- **Design Stance:** Pragmatic modernism enriched by micro-tactile surface response. Visual noise is aggressively eliminated in favor of ultra-crisp typographic hierarchy, punchy status feedback, and effortless single-thumb access zones.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate relationship between high-security institutional banking and lightspeed mobile consumer interactions.

### Role Mapping
- **Primary (`#1A56DB`):** The foundational brand anchor. Governs high-emphasis action triggers, active tab states, bottom navigation emphasis, and critical payment authorization steps.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Functional money-positive accent. Dedicated strictly to incoming funds, payment verification ticks, cashback vouchers, and positive transaction statuses. Paired with soft mint backing (`#D1FAE5`) to ensure effortless contrast against white surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`):** Dynamic electric royal blue for active focus rings, interactive card gradients, promotional callouts, and secondary link targets.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate):** Delivers clean structural balance. Replaces harsh neutral blacks with deep charcoal slates (`#0F172A`) for primary numerals, mid-tone slates (`#475569`) for metadata, and muted cool slates (`#F1F5F9` / `#F8FAFC`) for tactile container dividers.
- **Canvas Tones:** Pristine base white (`#FFFFFF`) with sky wash fills (`#EBF5FF`) for hero account modules, quick-pay contact circles, and elevated contextual cards.

## Typography

Typographic scale is optimized for high-velocity transactional reading. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides open letter apertures, balanced vertical heights, and high geometric clarity across numeric digits.

### Typographic Rules
- **Monetary Digits:** Set all financial balances, currency values, and OTP input fields with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to ensure tabular tabular alignment across transaction feeds and keypad readouts.
- **Negative Space Calibration:** Tighten line heights on large headline and currency displays (`1.15` to `1.2`) to eliminate vertical drift within sticky transfer sheets.
- **Hierarchy Stacking:** Pair heavy uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm` with letter-spacing `+0.04em`) with primary slate values to make transaction timestamps, status notes, and bank account masks effortlessly skimmable.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-centric fluid column framework built around single-handed thumb navigational arcs.

### Responsive Structure
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding. Action trays, quick-send contact bubbles, and bottom sheets snap directly to edge boundaries with comfortable tap targets.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margin. Main payment flows isolate into a centered `540px` high-focus utility channel, avoiding stretched keypad entry.
